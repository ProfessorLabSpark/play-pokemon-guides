# Contributing

Thanks for considering a contribution to Play! Pokémon Guides.

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repo (or, if you're a collaborator, branch directly).
2. Create a branch for your change: `git checkout -b docs/short-description`.
3. Make your change.
4. Open a pull request describing what changed and why.
5. Expect review and discussion before merge — especially on anything under `rfc/`, which exists specifically to be debated before it's treated as settled guidance.

## Style

- Match the existing tone and structure of the page you're editing.
- Keep guide pages focused and reasonably short — this repo is trending toward a multi-page book structure (see `professor-guides/`) where each page covers one topic.
- Cite sources for anything asserting official Play! Pokémon policy (see existing pages for the citation style).

## AI Usage Policy

AI tools are used in maintaining this repo, and that's fine within limits:

- **OK:** commit messages, PR titles/descriptions, grammar/spelling fixes, formatting, reorganizing existing text, and suggested edits that the human author reviews and accepts or rejects.
- **Not OK:** AI originating the substance of guide or reference content — new prose, filled-in gaps, invented explanations, or anything not traceable to something a human contributor actually wrote or explicitly directed.

The content of the guides — the facts, rulings, procedures, and opinions — needs to be human-originated. AI is a tool for editing and mechanics, not authorship.

## Questions or Disagreement

Open an issue, or comment directly on a pull request. Disagreement is welcome, particularly on anything in `rfc/`.
